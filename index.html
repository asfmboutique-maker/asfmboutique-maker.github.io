Corps Decode Studio

Terms of Service and Privacy Policy · Last updated: 25 November 2026

Corps Decode Studio is a private desktop publishing tool used by the creators of the educational video pages Corps Décodé (@corps.decode), Corps Décodé DZ (@corpsdecode.dz), Body Explained (@bodyexplained.ia) and Le Zoom (@lezoom_tv). It lets these creators upload their own original, AI-assisted science videos to their own TikTok accounts, after they log in with TikTok and give their consent.
