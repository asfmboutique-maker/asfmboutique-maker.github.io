1. Who can use the app

The app is used only by the owners of the accounts Corps Décodé, Corps Décodé DZ, Body Explained and Le Zoom, to publish their own content. It is not offered to the public and is not sold.

2. What the app does

  • Lets a creator sign in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorize the app.
  • Shows the creator's TikTok nickname and avatar before posting, so the creator knows which account will publish.
  • Uploads a video file chosen by the creator, with the caption, privacy level and interaction settings chosen by the creator, through the TikTok Content Posting API.
  • Labels content as AI-generated when it is.
